Privacy Policy

Gravity Sort · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gravity Sort (the "App") collects and how it is used. The App is developed by Pocket Hop Games, an independent developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (levels, stars, hints, lives and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent and can change your choice at any time.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them on a new device; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this App. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the App deletes it. To request deletion of data held by our advertising or purchase partners, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
